Peoria 1956 Address Finder

Look up how Peoria, Illinois house numbers changed in the city's 1956 street renumbering, effective May 11, 1956. Search by name, old address or new address. Each result links to its original change sheet.

Use the finder:** https://charliescarface.github.io/peoria-1956/

What's here

- `index.html` is the finder itself, about 15,500 listings.
- `Peoria-1956-change-sheets.pdf` is a searchable copy of the 712 pages of change sheets, including the correction and errata sheets and the key map.

Source

City of Peoria, Office of the City Engineer, street number change sheets effective May 11, 1956, with Correction Sheets #21–#29 and Errata Sheets 8 and 9 applied.

Not every 1956 address is included. The older streets along the Illinois River kept their numbers, the area north of McClure Avenue was renumbered in 1955, and some sheets may be missing from this collection.
